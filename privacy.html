Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

VIBIN AI LABS INC. ("VIBIN AI Labs", "we", "us") runs the company website at vibinlabs.si. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why, and what you can do about it.

This policy covers the company website only. Our products have their own policies. The Cadex web page and early access sign-up are covered by the Cadex Privacy Policy. Vibin is not yet available and will have its own policy before it launches.

Who we are and how to reach us

VIBIN AI LABS INC.
Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada
Privacy questions, access requests and complaints: info@vibinlabs.si

We have designated a Privacy Officer who is accountable for personal information under Canadian privacy law.

What we collect

This website has no forms and no accounts. We collect personal information only in these ways:

  • Emails you send us: your email address, your message, and anything you choose to include.
  • Hosting logs: our website host, GitHub, records visitors' IP addresses for security purposes.
  • Web fonts: the site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser information when you visit.

Why we collect it

We use what you send us to answer your questions, including questions about our products, partnerships or jobs, and to keep the website secure and working. We rely on your consent, which you give by emailing us. Under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act and British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act, we use your information only for these purposes. If we want to use it for something else, we will ask you first.

We will not add you to a mailing list because you emailed us.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or cross-site tracking tools. If that changes, we will update this policy first.

We use service providers to run this website and handle our email. They handle your information to provide their services to us, under their own terms and privacy policies.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing. Where your information is stored

Information collected through this website is stored and processed outside Canada, including in the United States. While it is there, it is subject to the laws of that country and may be accessible to its courts, law enforcement and regulators.

How long we keep it

  • Emails you send us: up to 24 months after our last exchange, unless we need them longer for a business relationship that follows.
  • Hosting and font logs: we do not control or have access to these. GitHub and Google keep them under their own policies.

How we protect it

We use access controls and encryption in transit, and we limit access to the people who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach of our security safeguards creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we will notify you and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy, ask us to correct it, or withdraw your consent and ask us to delete it, subject to any legal requirement to keep it. Email info@vibinlabs.si. We respond within 30 days and may ask you to confirm your identity first.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at priv.gc.ca or the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia at oipc.bc.ca.

Children

This website is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date. If a change affects how we use information you have already given us, we will ask for your consent first.
