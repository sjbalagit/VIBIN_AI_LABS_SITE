02 · Superintelligence Research

We study recursive self-improvement both with and without a boundary.

Direction A

Bounded Meta-RSI Systems

Self-improving systems with an explicit envelope. A meta-level improver edits a task agent's capabilities and its own improvement procedure, but only inside a fixed edit surface, checked by an independent verifier, and measured on benchmarks across domains.

Direction B

Open-ended RSI Systems

Open-ended search over agents, capabilities and improvement procedures: systems that keep generating novel problems and solutions without a fixed objective.

03 · ABOUT

Things about us

VIBIN AI LABS is incorporated in British Columbia, Canada. We are a small team of researchers and engineers who believe the next generation of labs will ship products and publish research from the same loop.

We work in the open where we can: our products are used by real people, our benchmarks and design documents are written to be read, and our research directions are stated plainly so they can be challenged.
