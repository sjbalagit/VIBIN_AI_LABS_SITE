02 · Superintelligence Research

Recursive self-improvement, studied with and without a boundary.

Two directions, one question: how do systems that modify their own improvement procedure behave, and what can be guaranteed about them?

Direction A

Bounded Meta-RSI Systems

Self-improving systems with an explicit envelope. A meta-level improver edits a task agent's capabilities and its own improvement procedure, but only inside a fixed edit surface, checked by an independent verifier, and measured on benchmarks across domains.

The question we care about: how much improvement is available when the system is required to stay auditable, and which constraints cost the least capability?

  • —Improver and verifier as a paired model system
  • —Restricted edit surfaces and what they preserve
  • —Gradient-free and structured weight-level search

Direction B

Open-ended Recursive Intelligence

What happens when the envelope is lifted. We study open-ended search over agents, capabilities and improvement procedures: systems that keep generating novel problems and solutions without a fixed objective.

Here the questions are about dynamics: what drives sustained novelty, where the process stalls or collapses, and which safety guarantees survive once the boundary is gone.

  • —Open-ended problem generation and curricula
  • —Transfer of improvements across domains
  • —Failure modes of unbounded self-modification
