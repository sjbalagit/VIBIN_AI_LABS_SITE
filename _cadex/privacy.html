What we collect

Information you give us:

  • Early access sign-up: your email address and the date and time you signed up. If you complete the next step, we also collect what your work involves, your province, and anything you type into an "Other" field.
  • Emails: your email address, your message, and anything you choose to include in it.

Information collected automatically:

  • Hosting logs: our website host, GitHub, records visitors' IP addresses for security purposes.
  • Web fonts: the page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser information when you visit.

We do not ask for sensitive information through this page. Please do not send us project documents, land records, title documents or anything containing other people's personal information through the page or by email at this stage.

Why we collect it, and your consent

We use this information to:

  • contact you about your early access request and tell you when Cadex is available to you
  • understand who is interested in Cadex, so we can plan the early access group
  • answer your questions
  • keep the page secure and working

We rely on your consent, which you give by submitting the form or emailing us. You can withdraw it at any time, as described under Your rights.

Under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act and British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act, we collect only what is reasonable for these purposes and use it only for the purposes described here. If we want to use your information for something else, we will ask you first.

Emails we send

If you sign up for early access, we will email you about your request, including an invitation when your workspace is ready. We will not send you other marketing unless you agree to it separately.

You can stop these emails at any time by emailing info@vibinlabs.si, and we will act on your request within 10 business days. We do not sell, rent or share your email address with anyone for their own marketing.
